Frequently Asked Questions

Where are 1st year B.Tech students accommodated? +
1st year boys reside in Chandragupta Bhawan and girls in Gargi Bhawan to ensure a ragging-free zone.
What items should I bring on reporting day? +
Beds and almirahs are provided. Bring bedsheets, pillow, blanket, lock, bucket, and personal items.
How do I apply for outstation leave or night pass? +
Apply through KIET ERP portal. Automated SMS/call approval is sent to parents before exit pass generation.
