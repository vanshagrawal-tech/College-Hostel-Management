Welcome to KIET Student Hostel

KIET Deemed to be University provides clean, safe, and comfortable hostel accommodation for boys and girls inside the campus. Our hostels provide a good study environment and healthy food for all students.


About Our Hostel

The hostel at KIET Deemed to be University is designed to be a second home for students. We have separate hostel blocks for first-year students, senior students, and girls. Each hostel has a dedicated warden, 24/7 security guards, power backup, and clean drinking water facilities.

Hostel Facilities

We provide the following facilities to all hostellers:

📶 Wi-Fi Internet

High speed Wi-Fi in all rooms and study halls for college projects.

🛡️ 24x7 Security

Security guards at all gates and common entry points round the clock.

📹 CCTV Cameras

CCTV cameras installed in corridors and common areas for safety.

Cold and normal RO drinking water coolers on every floor.

Washing machine facility and clothes drying area for students.

Hostel Rules & Regulations

  1. Gate Timings: All students must enter the hostel before 9:00 PM. Night attendance is taken daily.
  2. ID Card: College ID card is compulsory to enter the hostel campus.
  3. Visitors: Outside visitors and day-scholars are strictly not allowed inside student rooms.
  4. Cleanliness: Keep your room and corridors neat and clean. Throw garbage in dustbins only.
  5. Electrical Items: Heaters, induction cookers, and high-power appliances are strictly prohibited.
  6. Discipline: Ragging is strictly banned. Any misbehavior will lead to suspension from the hostel.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How are hostel rooms allotted?

A: Rooms are allotted based on seat availability and first-come first-served basis by the hostel warden.

Q: What should I bring when shifting to the hostel?

A: You should bring your mattress, bedsheets, pillow, bucket, mug, lock, and personal daily items.

Q: Is internet facility free?

A: Yes, high-speed Wi-Fi internet is provided free of cost to all registered hostel students.
